# V15.1 answer-key and label changes

Scope: `tests/grader/expected.json` (persona key, 331 pairs) and `tests/grader/v15_gold.json` (40 gold pairs).
No key or label was edited to pass a test. Every persona-key change comes from re-running one written rule set
(`tests/grader/rekey_v151.py`, the same `judge()` arithmetic as `build_fixtures.py` plus the rules below) over **all**
pairs; nothing was edited by hand. The rules are applied the same way in every field.

## A correction to the V15 report

In the V15 report I called the persona key "contradictory" (for example, a no-experience persona at a hard firm got
different bands in different fields). On re-reading, most of those differences follow from one rule applied to
different inputs: GPA (g37 has 3.8, g33 has 2.5), firm tier (tier 3 vs tier 4 thresholds), and class year. They are
consistent. Two inconsistencies were real, K1 and K2 below. A third rule (K3) was checked and changes no pair.

## Rules

- **K1: school credit only where the spec gives it weight.** The old key gave school credit (×0.5) in every field
  and full credit in quant. The V15 spec sets school weight to 0 outside IB/PE/consulting and halves it inside them,
  and the grader follows the spec. New rule: elite school is +1.0 and strong school is +0.5, only for finance/IB-type
  (`finance`) and markets (`sandt`) listings. Elsewhere it adds nothing.
- **K2: no relevant internship means no A.** With no in-field internship, the best band is B/C at any firm and in
  every field. Old pairs with band AB and zero relevant internships become BC. This applies across all fields, so a
  no-experience persona at a hard firm gets the same band in every field, moved only by GPA, tier and skills.
- **K3 (checked, no effect):** for an adjacent-field pair where the posting itself lists required majors and the
  persona's major isn't among them, take 1 more point off. "Business" counts accounting, finance, economics,
  marketing, supply chain and management, the same definition the grader uses. It moves no band: g39 stays BC.
  I left it in the script so the rule is visible.

## g39 and gold14/20/29/38: grader wrong or label wrong?

**g39_lesser_data at edf93e34fe (Baird "Internship – Software Developer (Year-Round)", tier 3), band BC. Was F 27.**
- **Label right.** g39 is a Statistics junior with a 3.7 GPA, SQL/ML/statistics skills and a real data-analyst internship.
  The listing names CS/engineering/IS majors and Java/algorithms skills, so this is a reach. But data → software is
  the adjacent move the key's rule allows (−1.5), and K3 (major screen) doesn't push it lower. A CS major with no
  experience at the same listing (g37, also BC) scored 54, so the grader ranked a stronger applicant 22 points
  lower. That is a grader inconsistency.
- **Grader fix.** Fit was charged twice: once inside the candidate score and again by the fit gate. For an adjacent
  major (config `roleGroups.adj`), or an adjacent major plus adjacent experience, the gate now uses a fit floor of
  75. An adjacent interest now costs 4 instead of 9 in the off-interest step.
- **Result.** F 27 → D- 43. That is still one letter under the band. I stopped there: any more tuning would be
  fitting this one pair (see open issues). g42 at the same listing: F 25 → F 36 (band CD, still a miss).

**gold14: Penn finance 3.8 with an IB internship, at Goldman "FICC and Equities – Sales and Trading" (tier 1). Label B–C. Was F 4.**
- **Label right.** A top-school, 3.8, IB-experienced junior is a credible S&T candidate. Tier 1 keeps it out of A.
- **Grader wrong, three real bugs:**
  1. The title matched the quant group ("trading") before the S&T group, so the grader asked for Python/C++.
  2. The quant title-major rule also caught "sales and trading".
  3. The source filed the posting under "Marketing & Sales", and the Finance & Banking interest didn't cover IB, PE or S&T.
- **Fixes.**
  - S&T group is checked before quant.
  - The quant title rule excludes "sales and/& trading".
  - Finance & Banking now maps to finance, IB, PE and S&T.
  - The title's role group now beats a wrong source category for interest and major fit.
- **Result.** In band.

**gold20: UVA economics 3.6 with a strategy-consulting internship, at Morgan Stanley "Parametric – Business Analyst Intern" (tier 2). Label B–C. Was F 23.**
- **Label right.** An economics major with consulting experience is a normal business-analyst applicant. Tier 2 keeps it off A.
- **Grader wrong.** Ops is in the consulting adjacency list, but interest and major fit used only the source
  category, and fit was double-charged in the gate.
- **Fixes.** Adjacent-group interest and major credit, checked both ways; the adjacent fit-gate floor.
- **Result.** F 23 → D- 41. Still one letter low (now off by 1, was off by 3). Open issue.

**gold29: Howard biology 3.5 with lab research, at Cardinal Health "PRN Pharmacy Intern". Label C–D. Was A- 86.**
- **Label right.** The posting text says pharmacy interns work "under the direct supervision of a licensed
  pharmacist" and must "maintain appropriate licenses". A state pharmacy-intern license requires PharmD enrollment,
  so a biology undergrad is a weak fit.
- **Grader wrong.** The posting matched the health group and the biology major as a full fit.
- **Fix.** New `titleMajors` rule: "pharmacy intern / pharmacist intern" expects pharmacy majors, and the role fit is
  capped at 20 otherwise. Rules can now set their own cap; the default stays 50.
- **Result.** C+ 65, in band.

**gold38: Rollins English sophomore 3.6, campus newspaper editor, picked Media & Communications, at Mercer University "Admissions Marketing Intern". Label B–C. Was F 25.**
- **Label right.** Writing and editing are core skills for admissions marketing, and the posting asks for social
  media, which the persona has.
- **Grader wrong.**
  - "Media & Communications" is a real category on the site (245 listings) but had no role-group mapping, so the
    persona's interest counted as off-field.
  - "English" had no major mapping.
- **Fixes.** Media & Communications maps to marketing, and English maps to marketing, the same as communications and
  journalism.
- **Result.** C+ 69, in band.
