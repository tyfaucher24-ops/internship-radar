# V14.3 research: where the resume-grading weights come from

This is the source for every weight and threshold the V14.3 grader extensions add to `match-core.js`.
The V13.3 base weights (fit 0.45, odds 0.40, prefs 0.15 in `match-weights.json`, the company tiers and the school tiers) are unchanged, and their sources are still in the V13.3 notes.
Checked 2026-10-23.

## Sources

## Decisions and where they come from

| Rule in match-core.js | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hard gates show **Not eligible** plus the exact reason, and the score is capped (`ineligibleCap` 35) | Class year not listed, citizenship required, or no sponsorship when the student needs it | These are stated requirements in the posting, not preferences. Nothing in S1–S3 suggests employers waive them. The reason quotes the posting's own field. |
| GPA is only a gate when the posting states a minimum | Otherwise GPA compares against a typical 3.0 | S1 and S2: fewer than half of employers screen by GPA (42.1%), and the usual cutoff is 3.0. Treating every posting as a 3.0 screen would penalize students at the 58% of employers that don't screen. |
| Missing data lowers **confidence**, not the grade | Confidence is high when nothing is missing, medium with 1–2 missing fields, low with 3 or more | We can't know how a missing field would score. Lowering the grade would invent a weakness. The grader test `missingDataDrops` enforces this, and the 20-pair check confirms a missing GPA never lowers a grade below what a 3.0 gets. |
| Factor order: major, then related experience or internship, then skills, then GPA | Top 3 positive and top 3 negative factors | This is the employer ranking in S1 (major 93.7% > industry experience 81.1% > industry internship 78.9% > …). School reputation (26.3%) is never a stated factor. |
| **Open-entry floor**: generalist operations or store-leadership roles, and ungrouped community roles, at tier-4/5 employers never score below 45 (D) for a different major. This does not apply to elite firms or when a hard gate fails. | `W.openEntry` defaults: floor 45, groups ['ops'], minTier 4 | S1: 69.5% of employers consider all majors. S3: 24.3% hire any major. For accessible, generalist roles, a different major is a fit gap, not a near-zero chance. This moved five V13.3 calibration pairs into their expected C–D band (personas from 90.3% to 91.8% in band) and created no monotonicity breaks. |
| **Fit × chance** ranking | `fitXchance = fit × odds / 100` | This ranks roles that are both relevant and winnable above roles that are only one of the two. It reuses the V13.3 fit and odds components, so no new weights are needed. |
| **Safety**: final score ≥ 75 and chance ≥ 60. **Target**: final score ≥ 60. **Reach**: below 60. | `MC.T143` | 75 is the B+ line and 60 is the C line in `match-weights.json` grades. S4's 71.8% offer rate is for students who already got the internship, so "safety" asks for a high chance on top of a B+ score. These tags never decide eligibility. |
| Realistic list = Target and Safety only. Top list = best fit × chance. Not-eligible listings never appear in either. | `MC.rank143` | This is the spec. |
| Confirmed fields only | `MC.confirmedOnly` drops any field listed in `profile.unconfirmed` before grading | The review screen fills the profile only on Confirm. Anything the user hasn't confirmed is never graded. |

## Honest limits

- The weights rank factors by how many employers *consider* them (S1). That is not a measured effect on offer rates, which no public source gives per factor.
- The 50 personas and their expected bands were written by the same author as the grader (V13.3), so 91.8% in band is a consistency check, not ground truth.
- One outlier remains and is reported as-is: `g39_lesser_data` × `edf93e34fe` (tier-3 mid-size bank, GPA 3.7, 1 relevant internship) is expected B–C but gets F (29). The persona's data internship doesn't register as related to this banking role. Fixing it would take a role-adjacency change that V14.3 doesn't make.
