# V15: Fairer grading, honest tags, faster Feedback (build spec)
Status: DRAFT for Radar BUILD. Evidence: `/workspace/qa-v15/REPORT.md`. Harness: `/workspace/qa-v15/harness/` (re-run against the build with `node harness/run_grades.js && node harness/analyze.js && node harness/targeted.js && node harness/match.js && node harness/feedback_quality.js && node harness/perf.js`; it loads code from `harness/lib.js` → point `ROOT` at the repo).
Constraints: no paid services, no new runtime dependencies, keep the CSP.

**About the numbers in this spec:** weights and thresholds are **judgment calls** informed by the sources in REPORT §3, unless a figure is attributed to a source.

## 1. Grading (`match-weights.json`, `match-core.js`)
### 1a. Lower school weight (ib, pe, consulting)
`v12.weights.{ib,pe,consulting}` = `{school:0.12, gpa:0.18, exp:0.38, skills:0.12, fit:0.20}`. Other groups: `school:0`, and add the old school weight to `exp`.
**Why:** NACE rates internships above school; school attended scored 2.6/5 (2022), and school reputation is a factor for only 26–28% of employers (2026). Target schools still really matter at elite banks and consultancies (Rivera; Reuters: Goldman hires about half of its juniors from target schools), so the weight is lowered, not removed.

### 1b. School scores
`school_tiers` scores: target 90, semi 70, **unlisted 55** (was 35).
**Blank school = 55 with `confidence:'low'`.** It must not be dropped and its weight re-spread. This removes the case where a blank school beats a listed one (72.2% of IB/consulting pairs today).

### 1c. Missing GPA
A blank GPA must not score like a 3.3.
- Score a blank GPA as **3.0** in the GPA part. NACE describes typical cut-offs as 3.0, and 42.1% of employers screen by GPA (Job Outlook 2026).
- Add the note: "Many employers screen by GPA; if a posting asks for it, leave it in."
- **Resume parser:** if no school line is found, still parse GPA and major from the education block. Today they are lost and the grade rises by up to +10.4.

### 1d. Tier-1 "standout" ceiling
Replace `schoolTier(C,'ib',P) target/semi || gpa≥3.7` with:
- **In-industry internship** (NACE's top factor) **and** (GPA ≥ 3.5 **or** 2+ relevant experiences).
- School may count as one alternative **only** for ib/pe/consulting.

### 1e. Experience realism
Projects, coursework and class assignments cap the Exp part at **65** ("related project") unless the project is paired with a real role. Only paid or unpaid roles, research positions and internships can reach "directly relevant" 90.
- Fixes: freshman → Neuralink A 90.
- Athletics stays an extra (≤ +6). It also stops counting as an experience entry unless it carries a role (captain, for example). Today, removing athletics costs up to 19 pts.

### 1f. Honest tags for estimated firms
When `tierOf(...).known === false` (97.8% of rows):
- Never tag **safety**. Use **target** at best.
- Show "Competitiveness unknown — estimated" on the card.
- Never show A+ unless the profile has an in-industry internship.

Also, `safety` requires: known tier 3–4, **and** chance pct ≥ 80, **and** the profile has prior relevant experience.

### 1g. GPA copy
"Strong" only at ≥ 0.4 above the expectation.

## 2. Feedback text (`feedback.js`, `match-core.js` feedback builders)
1. **Verdict template.** Never splice a full sentence into "worth it if you …". Use `verdict.why = "Worth applying if you'd relocate — it's outside your chosen locations."` (fixes `q013`/N1).
2. **Location sentence.** Delete "…, not a location you picked." The action becomes: "Outside your chosen locations — tick “open to relocating” if you'd move." Show it at most **once per page**, not on every card (43.7% of cards today).
3. **Skills line.** Use the posting-matched skill display names, de-duplicated. Never emit generic tokens such as "core". Format: "Matches: Python, Java. Missing: C++."
4. **One tag source.** The card's Reach/Realistic label must come from `MC.tag143` (today the card and v143 disagree on 10.8% of cards).
5. **Fit contradiction.** If the major is in the accepted list, say only "Your major (CS) is accepted." Drop "Posting asks for engineering majors —".
6. **Update the code comment.** "NACE Job Outlook 2026: 42.1% screen by GPA."
7. **School note (ib/pe/consulting only, when the school is unlisted).** "Big banks fill many seats from target schools (Goldman: about half of juniors, Reuters 2019), so network early: alumni, info sessions, diversity programs. Your internships matter more than your school in our score." Never show a school note in other fields.

## 3. Ranking (`MC.rank143`, app sort)
- **Hard filter:** class-year ineligible listings, with a CC 2nd-year ≡ Sophomore mapping, plus explicit CC handling. Also filter "(expected)" placeholders and listings older than 120 days in top and realistic lists.
- **Location:** chosen locations count +8 on the rank key, and remote +4 (judgment). Target top-10 in chosen locations ≥ 35% when ≥ 30 eligible listings exist there.
- **Diversity:** at most 2 per company in the top-10.
- **Strict field matching for SWE:** Network, power and race-engineering roles do not count as SWE. They may appear only after strict-field rows.

## 4. Performance: Feedback page under 2 s
1. Render the overall summary from existing `MSCORE`, set `data-ready`, and **then** run the what-ifs (`requestIdleCallback`/`setTimeout`), filling those blocks in when they finish.
2. Run what-ifs only on the user's top-N relevant rows (in-field plus top 1,500 by score). Estimate: about 7.8 s × 1,500/12,806 ≈ 0.9 s, off the critical path.
3. Remove the main-thread `scoreAll` duplicate; reuse `MSCORE`. Reuse the existing scoring worker instead of creating `_fbW` and re-posting `slimRows`.
4. Memoise firm-name normalisation (a `Map` keyed by raw string) and precompute normalised alias lists once at load. `norm` is 24.9% of CPU today.

## V15 addendum: Location and grade accuracy for choosing the right internships (Ty, Oct 24)
### Location
- Listing location must be accurate: parse into city, state, remote, hybrid, and multi-location (all offices listed, never just the first). Normalize city/state names and aliases (NYC means New York, NY). Unknown stays "Location not listed", never guessed.
- Audit: sample 200 listings against their live posting pages (respect robots.txt and skip blocked sites, noting them). Pass bar: ≥98% exact city/state, ≥99% remote/hybrid flag correct.
- Matching: the user's preferred locations (city, state, metro radius, remote OK, open to relocate) strongly drive the Top/Realistic ranking. With prefs set, ≥80% of the top 10 are in preferred locations or remote-OK (when enough eligible listings exist; otherwise fill with the nearest metro and label it "Outside your picks"). Never include a listing in a non-US location.
- Metro matching: use a vendored US metro/city lat-long table for radius matching (e.g. Charlotte includes nearby suburbs). No paid geocoding.
- No preferences set means location is neutral (no penalty, no boost). Tests cover remote, multi-city, and unlisted locations.
### Grade
- Each grade must reflect real fit: posting requirements (class year, GPA min, major, skills) plus profile plus company difficulty. Ineligible listings never appear in Top/Realistic.
- Add a hand-labeled gold set: 40 profile and listing pairs graded by careful manual review with written reasons (synthetic profiles, real listings). Pass bar: ≥90% within the expected grade band, 0 cases off by 2+ letters.
- Stability: identical inputs give identical grades, and small irrelevant changes (a name, the resume's formatting) change nothing.
- Recommendation quality bar: for the 169 test profiles, ≥95% of the top 10 are relevant, eligible for the user's class year, open, and have a direct apply link.
Report the location audit numbers, gold set results, and ranking metrics along with the other V15 bars.
