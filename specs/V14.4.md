# V14.4: Remove tutorial + review step, relocate and redesign Honest Feedback

Log as V14.4 in CHANGELOG. Never invent data. No layout shift. Light and dark modes, 390/768/1280px, axe with 0 issues.

## 1. Remove the tutorial (V14.2 part 2)
- Delete the guided tour completely: the trigger after profile build, the overlay and spotlight, the "Replay tutorial" item in the profile menu, the localStorage keys (clean up old keys on load), and its tests.
- Make sure no leftover overlay layer can block clicks (remember the starfield bug).

## 2. Remove the "check what we read" review screen (V14.3 section 4)
- After a resume upload, parsed fields go straight into the profile. No side-by-side review step, no yellow "check this" flags, and no source highlighting.
- Keep accuracy rules: never invent fields. Missing fields stay empty, never defaulted.
- Keep the preferences step (industries, roles, locations, season, remote) and "Build manually".
- The profile page stays fully editable afterward (edit, delete, add free text), so users can fix anything there.
- Keep the V14.3 input fixes (no cut-off text) and the resume accuracy CI.

## 3. Move the Honest Feedback entry point
- Remove the Honest Feedback button from the profile menu.
- Put it where people actually make decisions:
  (a) A main nav item "Feedback" (or "My Odds"), evenly spaced with the others.
  (b) A "Why this grade?" link on every internship card grade and in the detail panel, opening the feedback for that posting.
  (c) A small summary card on the home/For You page ("Your strongest area / biggest gap"), linking to the full page.
- Pick the cleanest combination. (b) is required.

## 4. Redesign the Honest Feedback page: simple, clear, animated
The current page is confusing. Rebuild it around 3 questions: How strong am I? What helps me? What should I do next?
- **Top: Overall snapshot.** One big animated score ring or grade, plus one plain sentence ("You're competitive for mid-size finance internships, but bulge-bracket banks are reaches right now.").
- **Animated factor bars** (same style as the grading bars, but cooler): GPA, Relevant experience, Leadership, Athletics, Skills and certifications, School. Bars fill on load with a staggered ease-out (about 600–900ms each), the number counts up, and colors run red to amber to green. Hover or tap a bar for a 1-line explanation quoted from their resume.
- **Strengths** (top 3) and **Gaps** (top 3) as clean cards, each citing the real item ("SMIF analyst role", "No Excel/modeling cert").
- **Next steps:** 1–3 concrete actions ranked by impact (e.g. "Get Bloomberg Market Concepts, free through most schools"), each with what it improves. Research-backed, no made-up stats or percentages.
- **Odds by tier:** reach, target, and safety counts from their saved and matched internships, with a link to see each list.
- **Per-posting view** (from "Why this grade?"): the same layout, but the bars compare the user to that posting's requirements, plus eligibility gates with plain reasons.
- Honest tone: long shots are called long shots. Short sentences, no jargon, no walls of text. Progressive disclosure means details sit behind "Show more".
- Animations respect prefers-reduced-motion (they show final states instantly). 60fps, no jank on phone.
- Empty state (no profile): "Upload your resume to see your honest feedback" plus an upload button.
