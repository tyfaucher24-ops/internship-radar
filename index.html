About Internship Radar

Internship Radar is a free website that tracks every open US internship it can verify, flags the ones about to open, keeps your applications in one spreadsheet, and shows the roles you have a real shot at.

Where the listings come from

Straight from company career sites, through the public job-board systems those sites use (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and others), checked again on every update; the header shows exactly when the last update ran. Public internship lists on GitHub only help us discover companies, and each lead is checked on the company's own posting before it appears. We never copy listings from LinkedIn, Indeed or Handshake.

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, no selling data. Deadlines and other details are shown only when the posting states them; otherwise you'll see "not listed".

Privacy

Everything you enter (profile, resume text, tracker, saved filters) is stored only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Resume files are read on your device and never uploaded.

Reports and "I saw one elsewhere" are only shared if you choose to open the public GitHub issue we prepare for you.

You can download everything with Export my data or delete it all with Clear everything on My Profile.
