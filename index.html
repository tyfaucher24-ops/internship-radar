STAR method

Situation: one sentence of context. Task: what you owned. Action: 2–3 specific things you did. Result: a number or concrete outcome, plus what you learned.

Quick tips

  • Keep answers 60–120 seconds; pause instead of using filler.
  • Research the team and one recent company headline before every call.
  • For coffee chats, ask about their path, not for a job. Ask for one referral or name at the end.
  • Send a thank-you within 24 hours, referencing one specific thing they said.
  • Phone screens: stand up, smile, keep your resume and questions in front of you.
