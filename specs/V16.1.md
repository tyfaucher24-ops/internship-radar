# V16.1 — "Sign in to save" button

Goal: guests shouldn't lose progress. Nudge them to sign in (magic link, existing V16 accounts) without nagging. Log in CHANGELOG as V16.1.

## 1. Persistent button (guests only)
- Top nav, right side, next to Feedback: a "Sign in to save" button. Opens the existing account/sign-in dialog.
- Once signed in, it becomes the normal account avatar/menu. Never shown to signed-in users.
- Mobile (390px): fits the nav without wrapping; shortens to "Save progress" or an icon + label if needed. No overlap or cutoff.

## 2. Contextual nudge
- Show a small non-blocking toast/snackbar the FIRST time a guest saves/tracks an internship, and again after they finish building the profile (resume upload or manual), whichever comes first counts once per trigger:
  "Saved on this device. Sign in to keep it everywhere." [Sign in to save] [Not now]
- "Not now" or dismiss: don't show the toast again for 7 days (store in localStorage). The nav button always stays.
- Never a blocking modal, never on first page load, never during the intro.

## 3. Sign-in dialog copy
- Headline: "Save your progress". One line: "We'll email you a sign-in link. No password. Your saved internships, tracker and profile carry over."
- Guest data merges on first sign-in (already built in V16) — after sign-in show a toast: "You're signed in. Your progress is saved."

## 4. Rules
- If accounts are unavailable (cloud-config empty or Supabase down), hide the button and toast entirely; guest mode unchanged.
- Match current theme (light/dark), no layout shift, accessible (keyboard, focus ring, aria-labels; axe passes).
- No new cost, no tracking beyond existing cookie-free analytics (count "save_prompt_shown/clicked/signed_in" if the V16 analytics events exist).

## Acceptance
- Guest: button visible desktop + mobile; toast appears once on first save; Not now suppresses 7 days; sign-in merges data with nothing lost.
- Signed-in: no button, no toast.
- Accounts off: nothing shows, site works as guest.
- All existing suites + axe pass; screenshots at 1440 and 390 in ops/screens/v16.1-*.png.
