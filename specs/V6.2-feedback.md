V6.2 HONEST FEEDBACK PAGE (Ty approved; build after V6.1). Log as V6.2.
After a profile is saved, an "Honest feedback" page (linked from My Profile and after Save & Search) reviews the user's profile/resume and tells them plainly where they stand. Tone: very harsh but honest and constructive, never insulting.
- Overall standing: grade distribution of their matches (how many A/B/C/D/F) and what that means.
- What's holding them back: specific gaps from their own parsed resume vs. requirements in real listings they're interested in (missing skills that appear often, no relevant experience, class year limits, GPA if given, etc.).
- Where they can realistically land now: role types and company kinds where their grades are highest, with links to top real listings.
- Top 2-3 highest-impact changes, each with an estimated grade lift computed by re-scoring with that change (e.g. "Adding SQL would raise 312 listings by one grade or more"), using only real listing data.
- Resume quality checks from the parsed text (missing dates, no quantified bullets, too short, no skills section), stated factually.
Everything is computed from real data and the scoring engine; no invented stats. Local only (localStorage), fast, matches site style; screenshots v62-*.png; test with the 3 test profiles.
