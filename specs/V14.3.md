# V14.3: Resume intelligence. Full resume scan, then grading and ranking of each student's best internships

Log as V14.3 in CHANGELOG. Start after V14.2.
Goal: a student uploads a resume, we read ALL of it accurately (every experience type and certification), and the grader in match-core.js uses it to rank their best internship opportunities, honestly and explainably.
Hard rules:
- Never invent data. Never guess a value that isn't on the resume. Unknown means "not found", not a default.
- $0 cost. Parse client-side, so the resume never leaves the device; store only structured fields in localStorage, and keep the raw file out of localStorage unless the user opts in.
- No paid APIs and no server upload.
- Research-backed weights only. Cite sources in specs/ref/v14.3-research.md and make up no numbers.

## 1. Upload and text extraction
- Accept PDF, DOCX, and TXT, plus paste-text as a fallback. Max 5MB.
- PDF: pdf.js (vendored). Rebuild reading order using text item x/y positions, so two-column layouts, sidebars, and tables don't interleave. Detect columns by x-clustering, then read each column top to bottom.
- DOCX: mammoth.js (vendored), keeping headings, bullets, and tables.
- Scanned or image-only PDF (almost no text layer): tell the user "This looks like an image, so we can't read it reliably. Upload a text PDF/DOCX or build manually." Optional Tesseract.js OCR behind a button, marked "may be less accurate" with every OCR field flagged for review.
- Normalize ligatures (ﬁ to fi), smart quotes, bullets (•▪◦–), hyphenated line breaks, non-breaking spaces, and unicode dashes.
- Strip headers and footers that repeat on every page.

## 2. Section detection
- Split into sections using a big heading synonym dictionary (case and punctuation insensitive, ALL CAPS or bold or larger font as signals):
  - Education.
  - Experience, Work Experience, Professional Experience, Employment, Internships, Relevant Experience.
  - Leadership, Activities, Extracurriculars, Involvement, Campus Involvement.
  - Athletics, Varsity Athletics.
  - Volunteer, Community Service.
  - Projects, Research, Publications.
  - Skills, Technical Skills, Languages.
  - Certifications, Licenses, Coursework, Relevant Coursework.
  - Honors, Awards.
  - Interests, Summary, Objective.
- Resumes with no headings: classify each block by content using the entity detectors below.
- Every extracted item keeps its source line(s) for highlighting in review, plus a confidence of high, medium, or low.

## 3. Entities to extract (all of them)
### Contact
Name, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, and city/state. Use these for display only; they are never used in grading.

### Education (multiple entries)
- School: match against a vendored list of US colleges (IPEDS names plus common aliases, e.g. "UNC" means the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, "Queens" means Queens University of Charlotte, using context). Fuzzy match. If ambiguous, ask the user to pick and never auto-pick.
- Degree (BS, BA, BBA, BSBA, MS, MBA, etc.), major(s), minor(s), and concentration.
- GPA: only capture an explicit GPA, plus a major GPA if present. Handle "3.92/4.0", "GPA: 3.9", and "3.92 cumulative". Never infer it. If the scale isn't 4.0, store the raw value and scale.
- Graduation month and year (handle "Expected May 2028", "Class of 2028", "2024–2028"). Derive class year (freshman through senior) from the grad date and today's date.
- Honors (Dean's List, cum laude, scholarships), relevant coursework, and study abroad.
- Test scores only if listed (SAT, ACT, GMAT, CFA Level).

### Experience. Classify every entry into a type:
- Internship, full-time or part-time job, co-op, research assistant, teaching assistant.
- Founder or own business, freelance, family business.
- Student-managed investment fund, case competitions, stock pitch competitions.
- Club leadership (with title: president, VP, treasurer, chair, founder) or member.
- Varsity athlete (D1, D2, D3, NAIA, club), with captain, years, and sport.
- Military/ROTC, volunteer, Greek life (with role), and publications or research papers.
- Hackathons, projects (personal or academic), and open source.
- Service jobs (server, retail, lifeguard), which count for work ethic and transferable skills.
For each entry capture:
- Organization, title, location, and start/end dates (handle "Present", "Summer 2025", "Jun–Aug 2025", and seasons). Compute duration in months.
- Bullets, kept verbatim.
- Derived signals:
  - Quantified impact (numbers, %, $), with the value kept.
  - Leadership verbs (led, founded, managed, captained).
  - Skills mentioned.
  - Industry of the org: use the company registry if it's there, otherwise "unknown", never guessed.
  - Org tier, only if the org is in our company tier registry.
  - Relevance to each field (finance, consulting, tech, marketing, etc.), from keyword plus registry evidence.
- Detect overlapping dates (fine) versus impossible dates (end before start), and flag the impossible ones.

### Certifications and licenses
Map to a canonical vendored dictionary with field relevance:
- Finance: Bloomberg Market Concepts (BMC), BIWS, Wall Street Prep, CFI FMVA, the Excel or Modeling cert, SIE, Series 7/63/65/79, and CFA levels.
- Accounting: CPA progress.
- Tech: AWS (CCP, SAA), Google Cloud, Azure, CompTIA, and Meta/IBM/Google certificates.
- Data: Google Data Analytics, Tableau, Microsoft PL-300.
- Marketing: Google Ads, HubSpot, Meta Blueprint, Google Analytics.
- Project management: PMP, CAPM, Scrum.
- Other: Six Sigma, Salesforce, Microsoft Office Specialist, language certs, lifeguard, EMT, CPR, OSHA.
Unknown certs are kept verbatim as "other" and still shown, but give no field credit unless the user tags them.

### Skills
Hard skills (Excel, VBA, Python, SQL, R, Tableau, Power BI, Capital IQ, FactSet, Bloomberg, PitchBook, Salesforce, Figma, Adobe, etc.), languages with proficiency, and soft skills (shown, but never used in grading). Normalize synonyms (MS Excel and Excel, JS and JavaScript).

### Other
Awards, publications, interests (display only), and work authorization if stated (the site is US-only; never infer citizenship).

## 4. Review and confirm screen (this replaces the broad profile build)
- Show a side-by-side view: the resume text on the left with highlights, and the extracted profile on the right as editable cards. Click a field to highlight its source line.
- Low- or medium-confidence fields get a yellow "check this" mark. Missing important fields (GPA, grad year, major) show "not found, add it?" and never a default.
- Everything is editable, deletable, and addable. Every list takes free text, with autocomplete from our dictionaries, so users are never limited to our options.
- Experience type is editable through a dropdown, so the user can fix the classification.
- After the resume, ask preferences it can't cover: target industries and roles, locations, season and year, remote, and willingness to relocate.
- Keep "Build manually" with the same fields.
- Fix the input bugs: no cut-off text in any input (full width, wrap, auto-growing textareas, long school names fit), in both themes and at 390px.
- Re-upload merges into the existing profile with a diff and never silently overwrites user edits.

## 5. Grading and ranking with the resume (match-core.js)
Extend the existing grader (V13.3 section 7: company difficulty tier, athlete and experience credit, honest feedback). Don't replace it.
- Build a candidate vector from confirmed fields only:
  - Academics: GPA versus the posting's stated minimum (a hard gate if stated), major fit versus the posting's required or preferred majors, and class-year eligibility versus the posting.
  - Relevant experience score per field, weighted by type, relevance, duration, recency, tier, and quantified impact.
  - Leadership score, and athletics (level, years, captain).
  - Certifications and skills matched against the posting's text requirements (parse each posting for required and preferred skills, certs, majors, GPA, and class year).
  - School: use the existing slight boost only if research-backed. Note it in the research file.
- Hard gates (a "Not eligible" label, not a low grade): the stated GPA minimum isn't met, the class year or grad window doesn't match, a required major is missing when the posting says it's required, and work authorization when stated. Show the exact reason.
- The grade per posting is A through F as now, plus a "Best matches" ranking: sort by (fit × realistic chance), give a Top Matches and a Realistic Matches list, and tag reaches, targets, and safeties honestly.
- Explainability: each grade shows its top 3 positive and top 3 negative factors, quoted from the resume and the posting ("Your SMIF analyst role matches 'investment research experience preferred'"), plus 1–2 concrete next steps. Use the honest feedback (7g) style, with no made-up stats.
- Missing data lowers confidence, not the grade: show "Grade confidence: low, add your GPA".

## 6. Accuracy test suite (required, CI)
- Build tests/resume-fixtures/ with at least 60 synthetic resumes, clearly labeled synthetic with no real people. Cover:
  - Formats: one-column, two-column, table-based, Canva-style, LaTeX (Jake's template), Word, and plain text.
  - Fields: finance, tech, consulting, marketing, engineering, nursing/health, and liberal arts.
  - Levels: freshman with only high school, transfer, athlete, international (US-authorized), career changer, and dense senior.
  - Edge cases: no GPA, weird date formats, no headings, multiple schools, and 2 pages.
  - Make each in PDF and DOCX.
- Each fixture has a hand-written expected.json (the ground truth).
- Metrics (field-level precision and recall), with pass bars:
  - School, degree, major, grad date, and GPA: at least 98% exact.
  - Experience entries detected: recall at least 95% and precision at least 95%.
  - Experience type classification: at least 90%.
  - Dates and duration: at least 95%.
  - Certifications normalized: at least 95%.
  - Skills: at least 90% F1.
  - Zero hallucinated fields: any field not in the ground truth fails CI.
- Grading tests:
  - Rerun the 50 V13.3 test students, now via resumes. In-band accuracy must not drop below 91.8%.
  - Add 20 resume and posting pairs with expected grade bands and gate outcomes.
  - Monotonicity tests: adding a relevant internship or cert never lowers a grade, and raising GPA never lowers a grade.
- Produce qa-v14.3/ACCURACY-REPORT.md with per-field metrics, failures, and fixes. Iterate until the bars pass. Report anything that can't pass honestly; never fudge it.
- Performance: parsing under 2s for a 2-page PDF on a mid laptop, with no UI freeze (use a Web Worker).
