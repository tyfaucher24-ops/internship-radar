# V11 — Newspaper Theme + Live Market Ticker (send after V10 is done)
Goal: make the site addictive to visit. It's currently bland. Keep it Simple, Functional, Accurate, Fast, Smooth, Visibly pleasing. Log as V11.

## 1. Newspaper *feel* (NOT a full re-theme)
- Ty: don't change the whole theme. Keep the current layout, colors, and components, and add an editorial feel through accents only.
- Apply proven patterns from the most-loved sites (see specs/V11-design-research.md).
- Editorial look: classic serif headlines (e.g., a Playfair/Old Standard-style display font) + clean sans or serif body, off-white paper background, ink-black text, thin rules/dividers, column layout, a masthead "Internship Radar" with date line ("Wednesday, Oct 21, 2026 · 10,8xx open internships · Updated 4 min ago").
- Front page sections like a paper: "Top Stories" (biggest new openings / closing soon), "Your Matches", "Just Opened", "Closing This Week", "Beginner Desk".
- Restrained accent color (one, e.g., deep red or navy) and grade colors still green-to-red. A dark "evening edition" mode is optional.
- Keep it modern and crisp, not cluttered or retro-gimmicky. Must stay readable and fast.
- Keep the planet intro, but restyle the transition into the masthead.

## 2. Live market ticker ("Internship Exchange")
- A scrolling ticker bar under the masthead, like a stock market: "▲ NEW Goldman Sachs · Summer Analyst · NYC", "▼ CLOSED Michelin · Supply Chain Intern", "● DEADLINE MOVED Deloitte → Nov 15".
- Only real events from actual sync diffs (added / removed / changed). Never fake or simulated activity. Show a timestamp per event.
- A "Market Activity" page/panel: live feed of today's changes, counts (▲ added, ▼ closed, changed), simple sparkline of open internships over time, top movers by industry/firm.
- Updates as new syncs land (poll a small diff JSON). Pause on hover. Lightweight: no FPS hit, respect reduced-motion.
- Click any ticker item to open the listing.

## 3. Requirements
- All V10 functionality and performance targets still hold (Lighthouse desktop ≥95, mobile ≥85).
- Accuracy first: ticker and counts come from verified sync data only.
- Done means screenshots at all sizes, a ticker demo, QA pass, and a report.

## 4. Design patterns to apply (from V11-design-research.md)
- Type: Inter (UI), Source Serif 4/Newsreader (titles), Playfair/Instrument Serif (masthead only), JetBrains Mono with tabular-nums for numbers.
- Warm newsprint light tones (#f7f4ec page, #1a1a1a ink), hairlines, double rule under the masthead, small labels above titles. No paper textures, drop caps, or multi-column text.
- Cards: thin borders, 2px lift on hover, serif company name, grade pill on the right. Cap the grid at about 4 columns.
- Robinhood-style rolling digits for counts; dim closed postings.
- Bloomberg-style ticker; add a colour-blind toggle (blue/red) for grades.
- Motion: only position and fade, under 500ms, plays when scrolled into view, respects reduced-motion.
- ⌘K quick search. 2–3 moments of personality: an "Offer" stamp in the tracker and a Morning/Evening edition dateline.
- Keep the planet intro as is (NO skip button; the Details button stays).
